Why most healthcare AI stalls

the real blockers
Clinical safety

No safety case, no deployment

A working prototype means nothing without a DCB0129/0160-compliant clinical safety case. Most AI vendors have never written one.

Interoperability

The data doesn't talk

Legacy PAS/EPR estates, HL7 v2 feeds and inconsistent FHIR maturity mean integration is usually the real project, not the AI itself.

Trust & adoption

Clinicians don't trust black boxes

Adoption fails when frontline staff can't see how or why an agent reached a recommendation, or where accountability sits.
